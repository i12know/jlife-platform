# Changelog

All notable changes to this project will be documented in this file.

This project is still pre-release. Until the first tagged release, dated entries describe meaningful repository milestones and MVP-facing changes merged to `main`.

The format follows the spirit of [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and this project intends to use semantic versioning once releases begin. The version ladder of record — pilot milestones as 0.x, v1.0.0 at pilot completion, post-1.0 majors as capability epochs — is defined in [docs/roadmap.md §Versioning](docs/roadmap.md#versioning).

## Unreleased

### Fixed

- **Consent and share-box copy now match what sharing does** (#120). The consent page said group members see "the parts you picked", but sharing posts the whole entry, Prayer included. It now says entries are private by default, ticking a group's box shares the whole entry including Prayer with that group's members with your name, and shares can be updated or removed on `/share`. `JLIFE_AUTH_CONSENT_VERSION` is bumped to `draft-2026-11-18`, so existing participants are asked to agree again (reminders are unaffected: they need only that consent exists). Vietnamese is a draft for translator review; wording awaits owner approval.
- **Participant screens are fully Vietnamese for Vietnamese readers** (#119, part 1). 76 strings had no Vietnamese draft, so Today, My Group, My Marathon and /share mixed in English: the navigation, the Day label, status lines ("Not started yet", "Draft saved"), the progress count, the reader and optional-readings labels, the My Marathon legend, the share page and several notices. All now have drafts (still marked DRAFT for translator review), and the nav names are translated ("Hôm nay", "Nhóm của tôi", "Marathon của tôi", "Đăng xuất"); strings that named "My Marathon" in Vietnamese now say "Marathon của tôi". A new test fails CI when any jlife-challenges string lacks a Vietnamese entry. Reading titles and references are still English pending the owner's content decision.
- **Vietnamese reading titles and book names** (#119, part 2). Vietnamese readers now see each day's title in Vietnamese and references with the traditional Protestant book names used by the 1926 Bible and the BPT reader ("Giăng 4:4-26", "Ma-thi-ơ", "Mác", "Lu-ca", "Công-vụ"; owner decision 2026-11-18). The plan build stores them under each day's `i18n.vi`; `jlife_studies_get_reading_day()` swaps the title and reference text for the reader's language and never touches the parsed references or Bible links, so English and every other language are unchanged. The build fails on a book with no Vietnamese name; the plan validator checks every day has a Vietnamese title with as many parts as the English one and a reference per passage.
- **Links are readable in dark mode** (#108). The participant screens set no link color, so links such as the Harmony Bible sections fell back to the browser's default dark blue on the dark background. Links now use the theme accent (light blue in dark mode); buttons, nav and chips keep their own colors. Stylesheet version bumped to 9 so phones fetch it.
- **`wp jlife group list` works** (#112). The organizer list command was registered as `list_` (the PHP method name, since `list` is reserved), so `wp jlife group list` failed with "not a registered subcommand"; it now carries `@subcommand list`. Found during the #112 staging check.

- **Solo readers and up to three groups per person** (#112, D14 changed by the owner on 2026-11-18, [#55](https://github.com/i12know/jlife-platform/issues/55#issuecomment-6983874900)). A participant is now **solo (no group) or in up to 3 groups per campaign**, minors too; the enrollment stays one per person per campaign, so consent, progress, entries and points are personal and **scoring never depends on groups**. Group membership moves to a new `group_members` table (person, group, role, status, joined/left); a fourth group is refused. The existing single-group enrollments migrate with an idempotent upgrade that never resurrects someone who left; `enrollments.group_id` is retired (kept as `DEFAULT 0` for one release, no longer written or read) and will be dropped later. The SOAP form now shows **one unticked checkbox per group, labelled with the group's name**, no "share with all", and none at all for a solo reader; a share reaches only the ticked groups, "already shared, tick and save to update" works per group, and an organizer hiding a post in one group does not block the others. `/share` has a section per group; `/group` shows a picker when you are in more than one group (one group's feed at a time, never another group's shares) and "Have a code? Join a group" for a solo reader or anyone with a free slot. `/join/?mode=solo` is **Read on my own**: no code, age band and consent as before, enrolled with no group (the "start a group" path is #112's next PR). Joins, leaves, moves and removals are audited, wrong codes are rate limited, D19 (minors share without a guardian step) holds per group, and entries saved before joining a group stay private unless shared. Organizer counts are per group (a person in two groups appears in both) and the score export lists a person's groups in one cell. Account deletion and the test-campaign remove command now clean up memberships. Matrix rows: design doc §4, §4a, §4b; covered by PHPUnit access-control tests written first. Also: an organizer-hidden post stays hidden when its author removes it (it could previously be removed and re-posted), and `test_share_failure_and_incomplete_flags_in_the_redirect` now turns the guardian opt-in on (it failed against the D19 default).

It now defaults to off; a deployment that needs a recorded guardian consent sets it to `'1'`. Sharing stays optional, author-chosen and limited to the participant's own group; private entries stay private. Covered by PHPUnit tests for the default and the opt-in.

- **One SOAP textbox, share on save, and a pluggable scoring engine** (#98). Today and `/soap?day=dNNN` now have a single **My SOAP** textbox prefilled with an editable `S — Scripture / O — Observation / A — Application / P — Prayer` template (Vietnamese translation shipped for the Vietnamese locale). Saving and drafting no longer depend on the text's structure; whether an entry earns the day's point is decided by a separate **scoring engine** (`includes/scoring-engine.php`): a deterministic `jlife_challenges_score_entry( $text, $context )` returning `passes`, kind translatable `reasons`, and the engine id and version. Each campaign chooses its engine (`campaigns.scoring_engine`, default `soap-regex`); the first engine passes an entry when S, O, A and P headings (`S:`, `S -`, `S — Scripture`, any case, English or Vietnamese words) appear in order with some text under each, and never the untouched template. **Submit SOAP** saves the text first, then scores it at once: a pass shows the green confirmation and the point (deadline, grace day and one-point-per-day rules unchanged); otherwise everything typed stays saved, no point is awarded and the page says what to add, and submitting again after editing can still earn it. Points are only ever added, never taken back, and the ledger records the engine and version that awarded each one. Entries, revisions and group posts are stored as one `body` text (existing test rows are joined under the four headings on upgrade; an older four-field local backup and a page still open on the old autosave are joined too, not lost). No AI or outside service reads entries; that needs its own privacy decision first (design doc §3a).
- **Share this with my group** replaces the separate "Share with my group or a chat" button on Today. The unchecked box sits between the textbox and **Submit SOAP** / **Save changes** and says it shares the whole entry, including Prayer. Ticked on a successful save, it posts the whole saved text to the author's current group (a later ticked save updates the same post; retries never duplicate it); unticked or on **Save draft** it never posts. The existing rules apply unchanged (current group only, minors awaiting guardian consent can't post, an organizer-hidden post is not re-posted); if the save worked but sharing didn't, the page says so and the entry and any point stand. `/share` still previews, updates and removes a post, and sends the entry to a chat, now for the whole entry. Matrix row: design doc §4a; covered by PHPUnit access-control tests written first.
- **Today** now shows the SOAP form directly (#96): the day's reading, then the collapsed **Read today's passages** reader directly above the four fields, with no "Write my SOAP" / "View or edit my SOAP" buttons to tap through. A new day offers **Submit SOAP** and **Save draft**; a completed day fills the fields with the saved entry and offers **Save changes**, with **Share with my group or a chat** still available. Submitting returns to Today with the confirmation; draft saving and the keep-my-text message for an incomplete submit work in place, and editing a submitted day keeps it at one point (the ledger's one row per person per day, covered by new PHPUnit tests). Today and `/soap?day=dNNN` (My Marathon and share links) now use one form renderer and one POST handler; the form carries its day, so one opened before midnight still saves to its own day. Sign out on Today returns to Today.

### Added

- **Daily reading reminder emails: queue, scheduler, sender and retries** (#63 Phase B, PR 2). A WP-Cron event every 5 minutes queues one email per opted-in, consented, enrolled person per campaign day at **06:00 campaign time** (Pacific, DST-correct) and sends up to 25 per tick through the Brevo transport; after a failure it retries at +10, +30 and +60 minutes (4 attempts), never at or after 12:00. Each email is in the person's reminder language (Vietnamese or English), with the day number, title, passage references, a link to Today and the signed unsubscribe link plus `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers; no Bible text, SOAP content, sign-in token or name. A new `reminder_sends` table (UNIQUE campaign/day/user) is the duplicate guard and the log: IDs, status (`queued|accepted|failed|skipped`), attempts and a fixed error class, never an address, subject, body or provider error. Everything is rechecked before each send (opted-out, left or unconsented people are skipped). Modes `off` (default), `dry_run`, `test` (only the private `JLIFE_REMINDER_TEST_RECIPIENTS` addresses) and `live`; WP-CLI `wp jlife reminders status|preview|run|mode`. A deploy sends nothing: the mode starts `off`. Runbook in `docs/staging-latoc.md`, sensitivity matrix section 4e. Vietnamese wording is a draft for translator review.
- **Staging restore test** (#56). `bin/deploy/latoc-restore-test.sh` restores the newest deploy backup into a scratch database through cPanel's `uapi`, compares table and row counts with live, and always deletes the scratch copy. First run on latoc, 2026-11-19: 31/31 tables, all compared row counts equal; recorded in `docs/staging-latoc.md`.
- **Daily reading reminders, part 1: choice, opt-in and one-click unsubscribe** (#63 Phase B; nothing is sent yet, the queue and scheduler are the next PR). A new `reminder_prefs` table (one row per person per campaign: opted in/out, language `vi` or `en_US`, the consent wording version; **no email address**; DB version 5) is purged with the account and with a removed test campaign. The join form has an **unticked** "Email me each morning's reading" box; ticked, it opts the person in, in the language chosen on the form. Anyone already enrolled sees a one-time "Get each morning's reading by email? Yes / No thanks" card on Today (either answer is remembered, so it never returns), and My Marathon has a switch and a language choice (defaulting to the account language). A public `/unsubscribe/?c=&u=&t=` page (signed token, no sign-in) shows a confirm button on GET and unsubscribes on POST, including a mail client's RFC 8058 one-click POST; a bad token shows one neutral page and changes nothing. Unsubscribing touches only the reminder row, never sign-in codes. The consent page now says reminders are optional (Vietnamese included); the sharing wording and `JLIFE_AUTH_CONSENT_VERSION` are untouched (#120). `jlife_challenges_reminder_recipients()` and `jlife_challenges_unsubscribe_url()` are ready for the sender. Matrix: design doc §4d; covered by PHPUnit tests written first.
- Added the opt-in Brevo SMTP transport for J-Life WordPress mail (#63 Phase A), private server configuration preserved across deploys, LATOC subject branding, redacted staff/CLI health checks, and a certificate-verifying SMTP probe. Missing settings or send failures do not silently use host mail while enabled. Fake-transport tests cover configuration, failures and site isolation. Live credential provisioning and inbox delivery remain deployment acceptance steps; daily reminders are separate.
- **Start a group** (adults only), **Leave group**, **Groups you left**, and **Vietnamese for the sign-in pages** (#112, part 2 of 3, stacked on part 1). An adult can start a group from `/join/?mode=start` (new sign-up: group name, then the emailed code) or from **Start a group** on `/group`: the group is made in the open campaign, the person becomes its `starter` member (it uses one of their 3 group slots), and the invite code and a full invite link (`/join/?code=...`) are shown **once**, with copy buttons. The starter can make a **new invite code** from `/group` (after a confirmation; the old code stops working at once) and holds **no extra view** of members' entries, progress or points. **A minor can never start a group**: no form is shown, and the server refuses it on the stored age band, so ticking "18 or older" on the form cannot override an existing 13–17 enrollment. Limits: at most **2 groups started per person per campaign** (counted over every status, so leaving or a disabled group does not free the count) and **5 creation attempts an hour**; names are plain text, 1–60 characters, and visible only to the group's members and organizers (an audit of IDs, never a public page). **Organizers** can list, rename, disable and re-enable any group, starter-made ones included, with `wp jlife group list|rename|disable|enable`; a disabled group's code stops working and its members lose the feed and the slot; re-enabling is refused if it would put a member over 3 groups. **Leave this group** on `/group` asks first (a confirmation page that works without JavaScript); leaving keeps the person's past shares visible to the group until they remove them, which the new **Groups you left** section on `/share` lets them do (their own old shares only). Every start, reset, rename, disable, leave and share removal is audited (D18). The database gains `groups.started_by` (DB version 4, added by the installer on deploy).
- **`/join` now shows what did not happen.** After the emailed code is checked, a person who is already in that group, already in 3 groups, or who tried to start a group as a minor (or over a limit) is signed in as before and now sees a short page saying why, with **Continue**; a new group's code and link are shown on the same page.
